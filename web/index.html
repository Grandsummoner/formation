NAVYARP2

A dual-scene probabilistic arpeggiator and step sequencer for the M-VAVE FM-1

NavyArp2 is a port of Navy Arp 2 (Grandsummoner): two scenes of eight step probabilities, a morph crossfader between them, six parameters with their own LFOs (Morph, Rest, Legato, Entropy, Harmony, Chaos), ARP and SEQ modes, Freeze, Latch, scales and roots, dice, 16 presets and three themes. It plays the FM-1's own voices and sends MIDI over USB at the same time. Read the manual.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Switch the FM-1 on and connect it straight to the computer with a USB cable that carries data. Close other music apps and tabs that might hold its MIDI port (the M-VAVE Workbench, a DAW, other installers).
  3. On the newest official firmware (V16)? Community installers cannot see it. Hold OCT- and OCT+ for 5 seconds to put the FM-1 in update mode, then go on.
  4. Press Install NavyArp2 and allow MIDI access when the browser asks.
  5. Wait until it says it is done. Do not unplug the FM-1 while it writes.

Or install a .fwsc file from your computer instead:

Going back to the official firmware, or to another one: the FM-1 Firmware Hub.
It does not start after an update? Hold OCT- while switching the FM-1 on (the rescue updater), then install again.
MIDI: USB MIDI out only in this version (the jack is a MIDI input; BLE is planned).

NavyArp2 is a community firmware, not made or supported by M-VAVE. Installing it is at your own risk: the author takes no responsibility for any damage or malfunction.
